## Open questions

Questions for the organisers (support+Buildathon-GDMxGCS@hack2skill.com):

- **Can they confirm the event through a Google or Hack2skill channel?** As of 2026-10-31, searches found no announcement outside startupbuildathon.com (research/notes/event.md). Registration and T&Cs do sit on hack2skill.com.
- Are non-Google coding tools allowed at the city round? The site names Google's tools; our workflow builds with Claude Code. Nothing found restricts this.
- Do participants get event credits? (A comparable event gave USD 300 and no more; Low confidence.)

- **Can we start building before 2026-11-20?** The site says "Build a new product", "Two days to build" and "Idea on day one", while the application already asks for an idea and an architecture. The T&Cs allow "a work based on an existing application" if we hold the rights. This decides how much of phases 4 to 6 can happen before the city round. Nothing published settles it.
- **Must the city-round product match the application idea?** "Pick your problem" on Day 1 suggests it may change; nothing states it.
- What time and timezone does the application close on 2026-11-13?
- Which T&Cs apply? The linked page is a generic template ("(Client Name)", no event name), but it says it overrides promotional material.
- Is the first-right-of-refusal clause really meant to apply here? It covers 6 months after the event, and it binds exclusive licences and sales of submitted materials (research/source-tnc.md, Intellectual Property Rights). This could matter to anyone raising money or selling the product.
- Does the team minimum or any per-city cap apply? The site says "No limit"; the T&Cs say "minimum and maximum team size" with no numbers.
- Who pays for travel to the Regional Showcase and Finale? Not stated.

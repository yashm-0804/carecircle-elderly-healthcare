# Prior art (India AI landscape, Gemini winners, gaps, funding): research notes

Startup Buildathon India 2026 · researched 2026-10-31 · by hack-researcher

Scope note: this is a landscape map, not an idea evaluation. Most evidence comes from web search summaries plus 4 pages fetched in full (Google Cloud winners blog, TechCrunch/Ringg, AI Vyuh, Winzheng/Accel). Many vertical entries come from search snippets or aggregator and press-release sites and are marked Med or Low. Several fetches failed (YourStory Gen AI Exchange list returned 403). Funding totals across trackers conflict (see Conflicts).

## Key findings
- Voice AI for enterprises is the most crowded funded India AI application segment: Ringg, Gnani, Bolna, Blue Machines, Arrowhead, Sarvam, Smallest.ai, Persistence Labs. Ringg handles about 20M call attempts a month and has customers such as Cred, Flipkart, Practo, Groww and Policybazaar (TechCrunch, 2026-08-25) [1] · Confidence: High
- Accel/Google India accelerator reviewers (4,000+ applications) said about 70% of rejected applications were "wrappers", with marketing automation and AI recruitment named as crowded; about 62% were productivity tools; the partner had hoped for more healthcare and education (Winzheng, 2026-03-16) [2] · Confidence: Med (secondary report)
- The Gemini Live Agent Challenge (11,878 participants, 1,536 projects, results 2026-05-16) was won by voice plus vision agents in physical or high-stakes settings: ORION (surgical co-pilot, Grand Prize), drone-copilot, Sankofa (voice + image + audio storytelling), Moonwalk (computer-control agent). Three honourable mentions involved India-linked builders or use cases: NagarDrishti (pothole voice reporting), Call My Parts (agent phones suppliers), Ekaette (phone and WhatsApp customer service) [3] · Confidence: High on list; capabilities are the fetch tool's inference
- Winners did not win on a generic chatbot. Every Live Agent Challenge winner is voice-first, and most add vision, screen control or tool use [3] · Confidence: High
- Earlier Gemini API Developer Competition (May to Aug 2024, winners Nov 2024) rewarded accessibility and specific user problems (Gaze Link for ALS patients, Prospera sales coach, Jayu assistant) [4] · Confidence: Med (older than 12 months)
- Indian AI app funding in the last 12 months clusters in: enterprise voice agents (Ringg $10M extension of a $15.5M Series A), AI edtech (Arivihan $10M, SpeakX $16M, ProLearn Rs 30 cr), credit and collections (Oolka $14M Series A, CredResolve, Rezolv $12.5M), legal (NYAI $1.5M, Lawyered $2.5M), tax and accounting (Prosperr $4M, Feb.ai $2M) [1][5][6][7][8][9][10][11] · Confidence: Med
- Government is building the vernacular voice rails itself: BHASHINI VoicERA (open-source voice stack, Feb 2026), Nyaya Setu (legal, Apr 2026), Samadhan Didi (grievance voice bot, May 2026), Kisan e-Mitra, Bharat-VISTAAR. This crowds any "generic government info voice bot" [12][13] · Confidence: Med
- Gemini Live API lists Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and English (India); the exact language count differs between doc pages [14] · Confidence: Med

### 2. Saturated segments and recurring hackathon ideas
- Saturated by funded players (judges will compare you to them): enterprise voice agents and outbound calling [1], AI recruitment and marketing automation (named by Accel partner) [2], AI tutors for exam prep and spoken English [8], debt collections [10], doctor-side clinical scribe [see healthcare row].
- "AI chatbot for X" and wrapper products are the pattern reviewers reject: about 70% of Accel/Google applications [2]. A hackathon-prep blog lists "AI chatbot for X" among ideas judges are tired of; it gives no data [15] · Low.
- I found no direct evidence of "ideas that appear in every Indian AI hackathon". The nearest evidence: at hackCBS 8.0 (Nov 2025, 15,800 registrations) a judge named multi-agent systems, autonomous decision-making and ethical AI as common themes [16] · Med. The Gen AI Exchange Hackathon 2025 (270,000+ developers, 4,400+ prototypes) winners spanned healthcare, governance, mental wellness, industrial optimisation and deepfake detection, so those themes are also likely crowded (Inference, Low) [17].
- Inference, Low: generic Hindi/vernacular chatbots, kirana voice billing, farmer advisory bots and AI tutors are well-trodden because government and funded players already ship them.

### 4. Real gaps (evidence-based)
Evidence of a gap is thin; each item states what evidence exists. None of these is a recommendation.
- Citizen and consumer-side vernacular voice agents with vision (point phone at a document, scheme form or bill). Evidence: the government is shipping voice rails (VoicERA, Nyaya Setu, Samadhan Didi) [12][13] but those are text/voice only; Gemini Live API supports Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali [14]; Live Agent Challenge winners show vision+voice works [3]. No India-specific vision+voice consumer product found in this search (absence of evidence, Low).
- Voice agents for small businesses rather than enterprises. Evidence: funded voice players target enterprises (Ringg customers are Cred, Flipkart, Practo etc.) [1]; kirana voice tools are hardware or app vendors with no funding data found [kirana row] · Low.
- Agents that phone or browse on the user's behalf. Evidence: Call My Parts won an honourable mention [3]; Ringg's own pivot is toward complex workflows [1]. No India-focused consumer version found · Low.
- Healthcare and education beyond what is funded: the Accel partner said he wanted more healthcare and education ideas [2]. Doctor-side scribes and exam-prep tutors are already funded (see table), so the open part is unclear · Low.
- Infrastructure gaps named by AI Vyuh: AI agent security testing, AI-generated code QA, agent cost management for INR-based teams [19]. The author sells these tools, so treat as marketing · Low.
- Real-time video/vision in Indian settings (surgery, drone, lab) won globally [3]; I found no India-specific industrial or field-worker vision-and-voice product in my searches · Low.

## Things that change how we compete
- Judges at city level see many hackathon projects; the Live Agent Challenge shows that high-stakes or physical settings plus real-time voice and vision outperformed chatbots [3]. Inference, Low: demo of live multimodality may differentiate.
- Business mentors and investors will compare with funded names. Be ready to say why you differ from Ringg, Gnani, Pype, Arivihan, Oolka.
- Government products (BHASHINI VoicERA, Kisan e-Mitra) are free incumbents in citizen services; a pitch must explain what they do not cover [12][13].
- Accel reviewers disliked "wrappers" that bolt a chatbot onto existing software without a new workflow [2].
- Equip already uses Gemini for live interviews [24], so using Gemini in hiring is not unique.
- Indian-builder entries won Live Agent Challenge honourable mentions (NagarDrishti, Call My Parts) [3], so Indian teams are competing in that format.

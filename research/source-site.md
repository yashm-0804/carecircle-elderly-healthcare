Who can apply for the Startup
Buildathon?
Early-stage startups with a new AI product
idea, aspiring entrepreneurs with an AI startup idea, solo founders, teams of two or more, and
anyone building an AI-powered solution with the intention of building business around it can
apply.
Do I need a technical co-founder or a
coding background?
No. Aspiring entrepreneurs with an idea are
welcome.
Do I have to pay any fees to participate
in the
Buildathon?
No, the program is free.
When is the last date for application
submission
for the Buildathon?
The last date for application submission is
13th Nov 2026.
How do I get confirmation for my
participation in
the Buildathon?
If you are shortlisted for the city
Buildathon,
you'll receive a confirmation email from us post the evaluation phase with further details.
What can I win?
The total prize pool is USD 100,000. Each
of
the top 10 teams wins USD 5,000 in cash and USD 5,000 in Google AI & Google Cloud credits.
How will the teams get
shortlisted?
The evaluation team will review the
founder's profile, potential of your product, idea, market viability, technical architecture and
use of Google AI and cloud infrastructure.
What is the maximum number of people we
can have in our team?
No limit
What happens after the city
Buildathon?
Top teams present at a Regional Showcase on
23, 25 or 27 November. Finalists go to the Grand Finale at Google, Bengaluru, in December.
Do we need to carry a laptop for the
Buildathon? How does the 2-day Buildathon look like?
Yes, you must bring a laptop to the
Buildathon. Day 1 focuses on ideation and design, while Day 2 focuses on building the product and
presenting it to industry experts. Further details will be shared with shortlisted startups.
